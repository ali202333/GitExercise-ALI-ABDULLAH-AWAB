How S-CoreBox Grading Works

Every grade you see on this site is built from aggregated critic and audience reviews, analyzed across multiple genres of filmmaking, then translated into a single, easy-to-read tier.

The Tier Scale

Every title and individual concept category receives a grade on this scale, from best to worst:

  • Z+
  • Z
  • S
  • A
  • B
  • C
  • D
  • EE
  • F

What Gets Graded

Every title on S-CoreBox receives an overall grade and a breakdown across five core film concepts:

  • Mise-en-scène
  • Cinematography
  • Sound
  • Narrative
  • Editing

Where the Data Comes From

S-CoreBox grades are built from aggregated reviews by critics covering our current library of the most reviewed films. Rather than relying on one critic's opinion, each category score reflects a consensus across the reviews available for that title. It works similarly to a Metascore-style system: more positive reviews contribute to a better grade.

A Note on IMDb Ratings

You'll also see an IMDb Rating alongside S-CoreBox's grade on each title. They are separate systems: IMDb's rating reflects audience scoring on a 10-point scale, while the S-CoreBox Z+ to F grade draws on the aggregated reviews described above.
