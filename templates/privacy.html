Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

S-CoreBox is made by the people. For the people.

Information We Collect

  • When you sign up, we collect your username, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Movies/shows you bookmark, and any ratings or reviews you submit while using an account.
  • Basic session information (used to keep you logged in) and a local theme preference (light/dark mode) stored in your browser.

How We Use This Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To let you bookmark titles and save your ratings/reviews
  • To keep you logged in during your session

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age required by applicable law in their region.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database associated with this project. S-CoreBox does not guarantee the same security standards as a commercial product, though reasonable measures (password hashing, standard web practices) are used.

Your Choices

You may request that your account and associated data be deleted by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the project develops. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
