Cookie & Local Storage Notice

Last updated: October 3, 2026

S-CoreBox uses a small amount of browser storage to make the site function properly.

Session Cookies

When you log in, we use a session cookie to keep you signed in as you navigate the site. This cookie is necessary for login to work and is cleared when you log out or your session expires.

Local Storage

We store your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage so your choice is remembered on your next visit. This information stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

No Tracking or Advertising Cookies

S-CoreBox does not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.
